1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to DoseSaathi ("we", "our", or "the App"), an open-source, privacy-first medication reminder and adherence companion developed by Shibu Dhara.

We believe personal health data is among the most sensitive information a person possesses. Unlike conventional medical applications that mandate cloud registrations and harvest telemetry for advertising or data brokers, DoseSaathi is built from the ground up as an offline-first utility.

Core Privacy Principle: DoseSaathi operates entirely on your personal device without requiring an account. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not monetize your data, and we do not display advertisements.

2. Information We Collect (and What We Do NOT Collect)

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use DoseSaathi to organize your medication regimens, the following information is generated and stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device:

  • Medication Details: Medicine name, dosage forms (tablets, drops, syrups, injections), strength, meal-relative instructions (before/with/after food), and current inventory counts.
  • Schedules & Reminders: Daily scheduled times, dose intervals, and cyclical schedules (e.g., 21-day cycles).
  • Adherence Logs: Timestamps when doses are confirmed taken, snoozed, or skipped.
  • Preferences: User-selected themes, audio chime preferences, and font contrast configurations.

Information We Never Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your real name, government ID, birthdate, email address, or phone number.
  • No Payment Details: DoseSaathi is free and open-source; no financial or credit card information is ever requested.
  • No Biometric or Location Data: The app never requests access to your GPS location, Bluetooth beacon tracking, or biometric sensors.

3. Android Device Permissions & Explicit Usage

To ensure reliable, unmissable medication alerts even when your phone is locked or running in low-power battery-saving modes, DoseSaathi requests specific system permissions on Android. Every requested permission is strictly used for core app functionality:

Permission Android Identifier Why It Is Required
Exact Alarms SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Guarantees medication reminders trigger at the precise minute scheduled, preventing delays caused by Android system battery optimizations.
Full-Screen Alert USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Enables the critical, full-screen medication confirmation screen to display over the lockscreen when a dose is due.
Post Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to display dose notifications, snooze prompts, and inventory low-stock refill reminders.
Wake CPU WAKE_LOCK Briefly wakes the device processor to play the reminder tone and vibrate when the alarm triggers while the screen is off.
Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps the active alarm ringing and responsive until you tap "Mark Taken", "Snooze", or "Skip".
Run at Startup RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Automatically reschedules your future medication reminders whenever your phone finishes restarting or rebooting.
Vibration VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback during alarms so users in noisy environments do not miss doses.
Battery Exemption REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Optional prompt to whitelist DoseSaathi from aggressive OEM battery killers (e.g. Xiaomi MIUI, OnePlus OxygenOS, Samsung OneUI) that silently kill background alarms.
Camera (Optional) CAMERA Used strictly on-demand to scan a family caregiver's pairing QR code. No photos or video streams are ever saved, stored, or transmitted.

4. Optional Family Caregiver Sync Feature

DoseSaathi includes an optional family link feature allowing caregivers to monitor adherence of elderly parents or loved ones. This feature operates with minimal data exposure:

  • Ephemeral Pairing Codes: Pairing is established using a temporary, 6-digit numeric code or QR code that automatically expires after 15 minutes. No permanent accounts, usernames, or passwords exist.
  • What is Synced: Only medicine adherence status timestamps (e.g., "Medicine A marked taken at 09:05 AM") and display names are synced between paired devices.
  • Serverless Edge Infrastructure: Ephemeral sync payloads are handled via serverless Cloudflare Workers with an encrypted Cloudflare D1 database.
  • User Control & Revocation: Either party can unpair the connection at any time in the app settings, which immediately purges the active pairing session.

5. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertisements

We take a strict stance against third-party commercial surveillance:

  • Zero Advertisements: DoseSaathi contains no advertising banners, interstitial videos, or sponsored links.
  • Zero Commercial Trackers: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or any commercial user tracking SDKs.
  • No Data Brokering: We will never sell, lease, trade, or share user health data with insurers, pharmaceutical companies, advertisers, or third-party data brokers.

6. Data Retention, Backup & Permanent Deletion

Because all your data lives directly on your phone:

  • Complete User Ownership: You retain complete ownership of your medication schedules and adherence history.
  • Instant Deletion: You can wipe all app data at any time by navigating to your Android Settings > Apps > DoseSaathi > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling permanently erases all local SQLite database records.
  • No Residual Cloud Copies: Because we do not maintain a permanent user database in the cloud, no remnant copies of your health schedules remain on any servers after uninstallation.

7. Data Security Measures

We employ robust technical safeguards to protect your information:

  • Local Database Sandboxing: Android's application sandbox prevents other installed apps from accessing DoseSaathi's local SQLite database.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): When utilizing the optional family sync feature, all payloads are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption.
  • Open Source Transparency: DoseSaathi's complete source code is public and inspectable by security researchers and community developers on GitHub.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

DoseSaathi is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child uses DoseSaathi to manage medications, it should only be done under the direct supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or caregiver.
